The original dataset can be found here: https://insideairbnb.com/get-the-data.
We download Ireland detailed data from 26 September, 2025 (listings.csv.gz, Detailed Listings data).
There is a zip file containing the dataset under `listings.csv`
That was the starting point for our data preparation and the file is included in this repository.

For this group project, we decide to organise create two different tracks:

- Consumer track
- Investor track

---

## Track 1 - Consumer

Based on a consumer experience to make a decision on where/how to find a good place to stay.

- Notebook file: `Airbnb_Customer.ipynb`
- Final clean dataset: `Ireland_Airbnb_Listing__Detailed__Customer.csv`

---

## Track 2 - Investor

Based on a investor experience to make a decision on where/how to find the best opportunities for investment.

- Notebook file: `Airbnb_Investor.ipynb`
